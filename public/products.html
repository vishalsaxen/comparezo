Where to buy

Upload products from Excel

Add many products at once. Put one store link per row with the columns Name, Image, URL and Price. Every product needs a Category (and its Sub-category, and Type where it has one): fill the Category columns, or pick one below for rows without it. You can also add MRP, Delivery, Store and Details. Rows with the same name become one product with several stores to compare. A product already on the site gets the new store links added to it. Excel (.xlsx) and CSV both work.

Where the sheet and the affiliate links come from

Names, photos and prices come from your sheet. EarnKaro and Admitad turn a store link into your commission link, but neither gives out a list of products with names, photos and prices, so those still come from you. Comparezo doesn't copy them from store pages either.

Download with affiliate links sends each URL in your sheet to EarnKaro (and to Admitad for stores your Admitad website has joined) and gives you back the same sheet with an "Affiliate link" column. You don't need it for the site itself: the Buy button already turns plain store links into your EarnKaro link when a shopper clicks.

ExtraPe has no API, and a Chrome extension can only run in your own browser, not inside a website. To use ExtraPe links, convert them with the ExtraPe extension while you browse a store, paste them in the URL column, and fill in the Store column (short links don't show which store they are for).

All products

Products are saved in Firebase. Edit opens the product's details under it so you can change any field (name, category, photo, stores, prices, links) and save. Delete removes a product from the site and the database. You can also delete a product from its page on the main site while this browser has the admin key. Copy list makes a backup copy.

Add deal

Add a deal or coupon you found, for example in the EarnKaro app. Paste the store's own deal page link: with EarnKaro on, the "Get deal" button turns it into your commission link. Deals show at the top of "Number of deals now" and under their category on Super Saver Deals, and disappear after their end date. Every deal needs a category; pick Whole store for a sale on everything.

Upload deals from Excel

Add many deals at once: one deal per row with the columns Title, URL and Category (a Comparezo category, or "Whole store"), plus Sub-category, Store, Coupon code, Details, Image and Ends on if you have them. Rows without a category use the one picked below. Dates can be 2026-12-31 or 31/12/2026. A deal with the same link and code as one already saved is skipped.

My deals

Edit changes a deal's title, category, store, code, link, details, photo or end date.

Needs a category

Deals from Admitad and Cuelinks come with the network's own category name, like "Women Ethnic Wear". Pick the Comparezo category for each name once and it is remembered for every deal with that name. Names that match a Comparezo category exactly are used straight away. Until a name is mapped, its deals still show under "Today's best deals". Your own deals saved before categories were required are listed here too.
