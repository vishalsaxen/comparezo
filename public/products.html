Upload products from Excel

Add many products at once. Put one store link per row with the columns Name, Image, URL and Price. You can also add MRP, Delivery, Store, Category, Sub-category, Type and Details. Rows with the same name become one product with several stores to compare. A product already on the site gets the new store links added to it. Excel (.xlsx) and CSV both work.

Where the sheet and the affiliate links come from

Names, photos and prices come from your sheet. EarnKaro and Admitad turn a store link into your commission link, but neither gives out a list of products with names, photos and prices, so those still come from you. Comparezo doesn't copy them from store pages either.

Download with affiliate links sends each URL in your sheet to EarnKaro (and to Admitad for stores your Admitad website has joined) and gives you back the same sheet with an "Affiliate link" column. You don't need it for the site itself: the Buy button already turns plain store links into your EarnKaro link when a shopper clicks.

ExtraPe has no API, and a Chrome extension can only run in your own browser, not inside a website. To use ExtraPe links, convert them with the ExtraPe extension while you browse a store, paste them in the URL column, and fill in the Store column (short links don't show which store they are for).

All products

Products are saved in Firebase. Delete removes a product from the site and the database. You can also delete a product from its page on the main site while this browser has the admin key. Copy list makes a backup copy.

Add deal

Add a deal or coupon you found, for example in the EarnKaro app. Paste the store's own deal page link: with EarnKaro on, the "Get deal" button turns it into your commission link. Deals show at the top of "Number of deals now" and disappear after their end date.

Upload deals from Excel

Add many deals at once: one deal per row with the columns Title and URL, plus Store, Coupon code, Details, Image and Ends on if you have them. Dates can be 2026-12-31 or 31/12/2026. A deal with the same link and code as one already saved is skipped.
